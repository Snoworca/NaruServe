## Acceptance Contracts

- SDS-AC-1 (FR-ENGINE-007 AC-1): WHEN BothAdaptersCases runs THE SYSTEM SHALL execute the same T07 body through TestApp and through TinyHttpd on listen(0) with a raw socket client sending Host → `BothAdaptersCases.t07Cases`
- SDS-AC-2 (FR-ENGINE-007 AC-2): WHEN a D-052 path, raw non-ASCII bytes or OPTIONS * is sent THE SYSTEM SHALL compare status only, because TinyHttpd may reject with its own plain-text 400 → `BothAdaptersCases.pathRejectionCases`
- SDS-AC-3 (FR-ENGINE-007 AC-3): WHEN /a//b with mergeSlashes(true) and /a%20b to /:x reach handlers THE SYSTEM SHALL record rawPath, path and param on both adapters and compare them → `BothAdaptersCases.handlerReachingCases`
- SDS-AC-4 (FR-ENGINE-009 AC-1): WHEN ServiceLoader runs on the NaruServe test class path THE SYSTEM SHALL find exactly DefaultAppFactory and RestApp.create() SHALL return a new NEW app per call → `DefaultAppFactory.create`
- SDS-AC-5 (FR-ENGINE-009 AC-2): WHEN the created app serves POST /echo with {"name":"a"} over a socket THE SYSTEM SHALL decode and res.json it back through TinyHttpdAdapter and GsonJsonCodec → `DefaultAppFactory.create`
- SDS-AC-6 (FR-ENGINE-009 AC-3): WHEN android.util.Log is absent THE SYSTEM SHALL choose ConsoleLogger, and through a loader with a fake android.util.Log SHALL choose LogcatLogger → `DefaultAppFactory.defaultLogger`
- SDS-AC-7 (FR-ENGINE-009 AC-4): WHEN the NaruServe jar is built THE SYSTEM SHALL contain naruserve-bootstrap.pro with the keep and keepnames lines → `DefaultAppFactory`
