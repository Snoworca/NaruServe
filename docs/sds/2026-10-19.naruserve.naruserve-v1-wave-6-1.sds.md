## Acceptance Contracts

- SDS-AC-1 (IR-MULTIPART-001 AC-1): WHEN the configurer throws THE SYSTEM SHALL propagate the exception and keep every earlier setting, because the configurer runs on a copy swapped in only after it returned → `MultipartSettings.copy`
- SDS-AC-2 (IR-MULTIPART-001 AC-3): WHEN a setter gets a value outside its range THE SYSTEM SHALL throw IllegalArgumentException "<setting> must be in <lo>..<hi>: <value>" from that setter → `maxFileSize`, `maxParts`, `memoryThreshold`
- SDS-AC-3 (IR-MULTIPART-001 AC-6): WHEN multipart is called outside NEW THE SYSTEM SHALL throw IllegalStateException before the configurer is invoked → `DefaultRestApp.multipart`
- SDS-AC-4 (IR-MULTIPART-001 AC-2): WHEN multipart is never called THE SYSTEM SHALL read java.io.tmpdir once at app construction, not at each parse → `MultipartSettings`
