feat(proxy): strict backend response parser sharing FramingLists

Move the backend response head parser into ResponseParser and the
chunked decoder into ChunkedBodyInputStream. The head is read as
ISO-8859-1 within a 64 KiB bound, the status line must be HTTP/1.<d>
with three digits in 100..599, obs-fold lines and bad names or values
are refused, interim 1xx heads other than 101 are skipped, and the body
framing reuses FramingLists with HEAD, 204 and 304 bodyless first. Every
malformed head is a ProxyException INVALID_RESPONSE; a malformed chunked
body fails the relayed stream with an IOException caused by one.

The handle-thread frame check of ProxyExchangeTest follows the head
parser to its new class.
